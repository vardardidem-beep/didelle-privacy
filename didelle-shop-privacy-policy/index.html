DIDELLE Shop Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

DIDELLE Shop uses the Pinterest API only to provide features you explicitly authorize for your own Pinterest account.

Data we access

When you connect Pinterest, DIDELLE Shop may access your account identifier and OAuth access token. These are used only to authenticate your connection and perform the requested Pinterest actions.

How we use data

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your Pinterest data for advertising or unrelated profiling.

Data retention and control

You can revoke DIDELLE Shop's access through Pinterest or remove your locally stored credentials at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact DIDELLE Shop through our official channels.
