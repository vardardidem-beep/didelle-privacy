Privacy Policy

AKI Creator Systems

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AKI Creator Systems accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when using Google APIs, including the YouTube Data API.

Google and YouTube API Services

AKI Creator Systems uses YouTube API Services to support authorized content management and publishing workflows for YouTube channels.

Access to YouTube accounts is granted only after the Google account holder provides authorization through Google's OAuth consent process.

Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, AKI Creator Systems may access information made available through YouTube API Services, including YouTube channel information, video information, playlists, and information required to upload, manage, or publish authorized content.

AKI Creator Systems only requests access necessary to provide its authorized YouTube management and publishing functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through Google and YouTube APIs is used solely to operate authorized content creation, channel management, upload, publishing, and related workflow functions.

Google user data is not sold to third parties or used for advertising.

Data Sharing

AKI Creator Systems does not sell Google user data. Information obtained through Google APIs is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the authorized service, comply with law, protect security, or where the user has explicitly authorized such sharing.

Data Storage and Security

AKI Creator Systems applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect credentials, authorization tokens, and other information used by its systems.

Access tokens and refresh tokens are used only for authorized API operations and are not intentionally made publicly available.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information obtained through Google APIs is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the authorized functionality or meet applicable legal and security requirements.

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of AKI Creator Systems by contacting us at the email address below.
