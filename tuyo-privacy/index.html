Tuyo YouTube Publisher Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This notice applies to the H02C Tuyo YouTube Publisher, operated by DIDELLE for the Tuyo YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube data we access

When an authorized channel manager connects the application, it may access the connected YouTube channel identity and basic channel metadata to verify the intended channel, plus the OAuth credentials needed to perform the requested YouTube action. The application requests only the YouTube scopes needed to verify the channel and upload videos when instructed by an authorized manager.

How we use data

We use this data only to authenticate the authorized connection, confirm the connected channel is Tuyo, and perform an explicitly requested YouTube publishing action. We do not sell Google or YouTube user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated profiling.

Storage, sharing, and retention

OAuth credentials are stored only in the authorized publisher runtime, not on this website. They are not shared with third parties except where required to provide the requested Google or YouTube service. Credentials are retained only while needed for the authorized connection and can be removed or revoked at any time.

Your controls

You may revoke the application's access through your Google Account security settings or request removal of the locally stored connection. Revoking access prevents further use of the connection until you authorize it again.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact DIDELLE via @didelleshop on X.
